Privacy Policy

TECH OPTION respects the privacy of its users. This policy explains how information is handled when you interact with TECH OPTION services or its Telegram bot.

Information We May Process

  • Telegram User ID
  • Display name and username, if available
  • Bot interaction and subscription status
  • Subscription start and expiration dates
  • Requests and actions submitted through the bot
  • Technical logs required for security, troubleshooting, and service operation

How We Use It

  • Manage subscriptions and service access
  • Provide requested bot functionality and support
  • Maintain reliability and security
  • Prevent abuse, spam, or unauthorized access
  • Diagnose technical problems

TECH OPTION does not sell users' personal information. We do not request Telegram passwords or Telegram account credentials.
